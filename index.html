Towa Shixun Huang (黄诗迅)

I'm a Computer Science PhD student at the University of British Columbia, advised by Peter Yichen Chen. I received my BSc in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Toronto, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professors Jude Kong and Eitan Grinspun. Previously, I was a member of the DGP Lab, where I worked with Yue Chang, a wonderful mentor and friend.

I work on Physics AI: integrating physical principles with learning methods to build scalable, stable, and generalizable models via data-assisted optimization of differential/integral equations that preserve physical structure.
